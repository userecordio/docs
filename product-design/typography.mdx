Every value below comes from the Figma variables collection and matches a standard Tailwind class. Use the class, not the pixel value.

```tsx
<h2 className="text-2xl font-semibold tracking-tight">Overview</h2>
<p className="text-sm text-muted-foreground">Last 30 days</p>
```
